Voices for the Baker cast

Thirteen people, two or three candidate voices each, every one speaking that character's own lines in the mood the script gives the line. One is marked as recommended. Listen, tick the ones you like, and say your picks in chat.

Read this first. These are real samples, made on this computer with the same voice model and the same tool the game uses, so what you hear is what the game would play. But I cannot hear. My recommendations come from measuring each voice (how high, how bright, how fast, how much it rises and falls) and from what each voice is known to be like. Treat them as a shortlist in a sensible order, and trust your ear over my tick.

How a resident gets a voice today

What exists

    What can be tuned

    • Shift: raises or lowers pitch and the size of the voice together. Below 1 sounds bigger and so a little older; above 1 smaller and younger. The town's elders are at 0.92 to 0.93, its children at 1.24 to 1.28.
    • Speed: the pace.
    • Accent rules: American or British pronunciation.
    • Mood, per line: six settings (neutral, happy, sad, angry, surprised, thinking) that change pitch, pace, tone, loudness and punctuation. Never the words.

    That is all. There is no setting for age, roughness, breath or accent strength.
