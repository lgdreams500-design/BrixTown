# Scenarios

Short stories used to find features. Each line in brackets is a feature in `features.json`. Add a
scenario whenever an idea comes up; if it needs something not in the backlog, add that feature too.

## A player's first hour
You step off the tram in the old town at eight in the morning [F-VEH-TRANSIT, F-TIME-DAY]. The street
is busy: a courier hurries past with a parcel, a shopkeeper is setting out crates, an elder is on
her way to the garden [F-NPC-POP, F-NPC-ROUTINE, F-OBJ-CARRY]. You are hungry, so you buy bread at
the counter [F-BLD-SHOP, F-OBJ-POINTS] and eat it on a bench that someone else is already sitting on
[F-OBJ-POINTS]. A notice at the workshop offers a job; you go in and do a first task [F-BLD-WORK,
F-BLD-INSIDE]. It starts to rain; people leave the garden for the café and lamps come on early
[F-WEATHER, F-NPC-REACT]. You follow them in and get talking [F-NPC-TALK].

## A shopkeeper's Tuesday
Up at six above the shop [F-MAP-LOTS]. Opens at seven, takes a delivery from the van at half past
[F-VEH-TRAFFIC, F-OBJ-CARRY]. Serves until one, closes for lunch at the café [F-NPC-ROUTINE]. Back
until six. Tuesday is not market day, so the evening is a walk round the garden; on Saturday it would
be the market stall instead [F-NPC-WEEK, F-GARDEN]. Home by eight, lights on, asleep by eleven
[F-TIME-DAY]. Nobody was watching for most of this: it only had to be true when a player came by
[F-NPC-LOAD].

## One street across five years
The saplings planted in the square the day the server started are head-high after a year and shade the
benches by year four [F-VEG-GROW]. Each spring the plum trees blossom a week before the planes leaf;
each autumn the square turns ochre and then bare [F-SEASONS, F-VEG-LEAF]. In year three the old plane
by the clinic comes down and the gardener puts a young one in its place [F-VEG-LIFE]. The empty lot on
the corner becomes a second café [F-MAP-LOTS]. Cats have moved into the depot yard [F-ANIMALS,
F-BLD-UTILITY].

## A bad day
You are ill. The clinic is across town, so you take the tram [F-BLD-HOSPITAL, F-VEH-TRANSIT]. The
waiting room has four chairs and three are taken [F-OBJ-POINTS]. A storm has brought a branch down
across the lane and a utility crew is clearing it [F-WEATHER, F-VEG-LIFE, F-BLD-UTILITY]. On the way
home you cut through the arcade because it is dry [F-BLD-MALL].
