## What each level means

**City layout** (`F-MAP-CITY`). How much city there is to walk around.
- 0 One block: One hand-built city block with streets around it. Size M.
- 1 A district: Several blocks laid out from lot data, loaded as you move. Size L.
- 2 Whole city: Districts with their own character, streamed seamlessly. Size XL.

**Lots and building records** (`F-MAP-LOTS`). Every plot knows what it is, who owns it and when it is open.
- 0 Hand-placed: Buildings are placed by hand with no data behind them. Size S.
- 1 Registry: A data record per lot drives signs, opening hours, owners and who lives or works there. Size M.
- 2 Changing lots: Lots can change use, be built on or stand empty over time. Size L.

**Going inside buildings** (`F-BLD-INSIDE`). Whether doors lead anywhere.
- 0 Fronts only: Buildings are fronts; people step in and vanish. Size S.
- 1 Key rooms: A few important buildings have one room you can enter. Size L.
- 2 Most buildings: Most buildings have furnished interiors. Size XL.

**Shops and market** (`F-BLD-SHOP`). Where money gets spent.
- 0 Decoration: Stalls and shop fronts you cannot use. Size S.
- 1 Buy at a counter: A shopkeeper sells a short list of goods through the ledger. Size M.
- 2 Stock and prices: Shops hold stock, change prices and can be run by players. Size XL.

**Mall** (`F-BLD-MALL`). A big indoor place to shop and meet.
- 0 None: No mall; shops are on the street. Size -.
- 1 Arcade: One covered arcade with four shops and seating. Size L.
- 2 Full mall: Several floors, many shops, events and crowds. Size XL.

**Hospital and clinic** (`F-BLD-HOSPITAL`). Where health is restored.
- 0 A door: A clinic front you cannot enter. Size S.
- 1 Clinic room: One room where a nurse restores health for a fee. Size M.
- 2 Hospital: A staffed hospital with wards, a medical career and an ambulance. Size XL.

**Bank, showroom, library, museum, cinema** (`F-BLD-CIVIC`). Places for errands and evenings out beyond shops and cafes, and where money, cars and culture will later live.
- 0 None: No such places; the town has shops, cafes, a mall and a hospital. Size -.
- 1 Places to visit: A bank, a car showroom, a library, a museum and a cinema (plus a second hospital and mall), each with its own front and furnished interior, staff at work and residents visiting in their day. Size M.
- 2 Working places: They work: accounts and loans at the bank through the ledger, buying a car at the showroom, films on a timetable, exhibitions that change. Size XL.

**Workplaces** (`F-BLD-WORK`). Where careers happen.
- 0 Fronts: Offices and workshops are fronts; workers go in and out. Size S.
- 1 One job site: One workplace with real tasks for the player. Size L.
- 2 Several careers: Different workplaces for each career track. Size XL.

**Utility areas** (`F-BLD-UTILITY`). Power, water, waste and depots.
- 0 Set dressing: A depot and pipes as scenery. Size S.
- 1 Bills and workers: Utility workers do rounds; lots get utility bills. Size M.
- 2 Outages and repairs: Things break; districts feel it; repair is a job. Size L.

**Gardens and parks** (`F-GARDEN`). Green places to rest and meet.
- 0 Static garden: A garden with paths and plants that never change. Size S.
- 1 Living garden: Benches people use, seasonal plants, a gardener at work. Size M.
- 2 Community garden: Players plant and tend plots; garden events. Size L.

**Population** (`F-NPC-POP`). How many residents there are and how varied.
- 0 A handful: Five or six people standing at their posts. Size S.
- 1 A neighbourhood: 20 to 30 residents per block, each with a home, a role and a look. Size M.
- 2 A crowd: Hundreds across the city, with cheaper far-away versions. Size XL.

**Daily routines** (`F-NPC-ROUTINE`). What residents do with their day.
- 0 At a post: Each person stays in one place. Size S.
- 1 By the clock: Home, work, meals and leisure by the time of day, a little different each day. Size M.
- 2 By their needs: People choose what to do from their own needs, money and mood. Size XL.

**Week and calendar** (`F-NPC-WEEK`). Whether Tuesday differs from Sunday.
- 0 Every day the same: One routine for every day. Size -.
- 1 Weekdays and weekends: Work days, rest days and market days differ. Size S.
- 2 Festivals: Holidays and seasonal events change the whole town. Size L.

**Loading people as you arrive** (`F-NPC-LOAD`). Residents appear where their routine says, only near players.
- 0 Always there: Everyone is always loaded. Fine for one block. Size -.
- 1 Placed by routine: People are placed from their routine when a player comes near and removed when all leave. Size M.
- 2 Distance tiers: Full, simple and data-only versions by distance, within a server budget, for many players. Size L.

**Talking with residents** (`F-NPC-TALK`). What happens when you speak to someone.
- 0 Greetings: People say a line as you pass. Size S.
- 1 Dialogue: Conversations with choices and outcomes, standing or walking. Size L.
- 2 Residents talk to each other: People chat among themselves and news spreads. Size L.

**Faces while talking** (`F-NPC-SPEECH`). Whether a speaker's mouth and face move with what they say.
- 0 Still faces: The line shows as text; the face keeps its resting mouth. Size -.
- 1 Talking mouth: The speaker's mouth opens and closes in the rhythm of the line's text while it shows, and the face takes the line's mood. Size M.
- 2 Lip sync: Mouth shapes timed to each sound of the line (from its text, or from voice if Q-14 says so), expressions blended over time, nods and glances on stressed words. Size L.

**Reacting to the world** (`F-NPC-REACT`). Whether people notice rain, night and you.
- 0 No reaction: People ignore weather and time. Size -.
- 1 Weather and night: People shelter from rain, lamps and windows light up, people glance at you. Size M.
- 2 Events: People gather, flee or help when something happens. Size L.

- 0 Pushed aside: A car that reaches a resident stops and the resident is pushed aside (CITY-064 as written). Size -.
- 1 Dodge: Residents see a car heading at them and scramble out of its path: a hurried sidestep or jump to the side, then carry on. Size S.
- 2 Dodge and tell off: Once clear, they turn on the driver: a raised fist or arms flung up and a short shout (a speech tag, no words spoken, Q-14), then go back to what they were doing. Size S.

- 2 Pets: Animals with owners, routines and needs. Size L.

- 1 Shared: Residents and players share the same objects, each with a number of places. Size M.
- 2 Objects with a state: Things get dirty, empty or broken and someone has to fix them. Size L.

**Carrying and moving things** (`F-OBJ-CARRY`). Deliveries, shopping bags, things left where you put them.

- 2 Things stay moved: Anything a player or resident moves stays there for everyone. Size L.

- 0 None: No public transport. Size -.
- 1 One line: One tram line on a timetable; residents use it to leave and return; players ride it. Size L.
- 2 Network: Several lines across the city. Size L.

- 0 Walking only: Players walk. Size -.
- 1 Bicycle or scooter: A light vehicle to own and ride. Size L.
- 2 Cars: Cars to buy, drive and park. Size XL.

- 0 Fixed hour: Always the same time of day. Size -.
- 1 Clock-driven light: Sun, dusk, lanterns and lit windows follow the world clock. Size M.
- 2 Night life: The town works differently after dark: night shops, quiet streets. Size M.

**Weather** (`F-WEATHER`). Rain, cloud and fog shared by everyone.
- 0 Always clear: No weather. Size -.
- 1 Changing weather: Clear, cloud, rain and fog, the same for every player. Size M.

**Seasons** (`F-SEASONS`). A year that turns.
- 0 One season: Always summer. Size -.
- 1 Four seasons: Light, weather odds and plants change through the year. Size M.
- 2 Seasonal life: Seasonal goods, prices and activities. Size L.

**Plant and tree growth** (`F-VEG-GROW`). Plants get bigger over the years.
- 0 Static: Plants never change size. Size -.
- 1 Growth from age: Size is worked out from how long ago something was planted. Size M.
- 2 Planting and tending: Players plant, water and harvest on land they own. Size L.

**Spring, autumn and leaf fall** (`F-VEG-LEAF`). Buds, blossom, autumn colour and bare branches.
- 0 Evergreen: Leaves never change. Size -.
- 1 Leaf cycle: Each kind of tree buds, blossoms, turns and drops its leaves on its own dates. Size M.
- 2 Leaves on the ground: Fallen leaves pile up and are swept away by residents. Size M.

**Trees falling and being replanted** (`F-VEG-LIFE`). Old or storm-hit trees come down and new ones go in.
- 0 Trees are forever: Nothing ever happens to a tree. Size -.
- 1 Fall and replant: Trees reach the end of their life, are cleared and replanted by the town. Size S.
- 2 Storm damage: Storms bring trees down across paths; clearing them is a job. Size L.

**Sounds of the city** (`F-SND-WORLD`). What you hear around you: engines, the tram, voices, birds, doors.
- 0 Ambience bed: One looping street ambience that changes with time of day and indoors or outdoors. Size S.
- 1 Things make sound: Each nearby car, tram, resident and door makes its own sound from where it is: engine pitch follows speed, the tram hums and rings its bell at stops, residents murmur when they talk (no words, Q-14), footsteps, birds by day. Size M.
- 2 Full mix: Sounds follow the world: rain and wind with the weather, echo indoors and in streets, a mix that stays clear in crowds, and each kind of building with its own room sound. Size L.

**Interaction sounds** (`F-SND-ACTION`). Feedback when you do something: open a door, sit, use an object, drive.
- 0 Menu clicks: Sounds for the interface only: prompts, choices, money in and out. Size S.
- 1 Every action: Every interaction point and the player car have their own sound: doors, chairs, taps, the car door, engine start, horn, skids. Size M.
- 2 Made by creators: Creators can attach their own sounds to items they make, checked like other user content. Size M.

**Music** (`F-SND-MUSIC`). Mood, and music as something people play and listen to.
- 0 Background score: A soft score that changes with time of day; mutes in conversations. Size S.
- 1 Music in the world: Music comes from places: car radio with a few music stations and podcasts, shop and café music, a busker; the score fades when a source is near. Size M.
- 2 Players make music: Instruments and speakers players own and play for others, heard by everyone nearby. Size L.
