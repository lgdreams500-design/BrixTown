## What each level means

**City layout** (`F-MAP-CITY`). How much city there is to walk around.
- 0 One block: One hand-built city block with streets around it. Size M.
- 1 A district: Several blocks laid out from lot data, loaded as you move. Size L.
- 2 Whole city: Districts with their own character, streamed seamlessly. Size XL.

**Lots and building records** (`F-MAP-LOTS`). Every plot knows what it is, who owns it and when it is open.
- 0 Hand-placed: Buildings are placed by hand with no data behind them. Size S.
- 1 Registry: A data record per lot drives signs, opening hours, owners and who lives or works there. Size M.
- 2 Changing lots: Lots can change use, be built on or stand empty over time. Size L.

**Going inside buildings** (`F-BLD-INSIDE`). Whether doors lead anywhere.
- 0 Fronts only: Buildings are fronts; people step in and vanish. Size S.
- 1 Key rooms: A few important buildings have one room you can enter. Size L.
- 2 Most buildings: Most buildings have furnished interiors. Size XL.

**Shops and market** (`F-BLD-SHOP`). Where money gets spent.
- 0 Decoration: Stalls and shop fronts you cannot use. Size S.
- 1 Buy at a counter: A shopkeeper sells a short list of goods through the ledger. Size M.
- 2 Stock and prices: Shops hold stock, change prices and can be run by players. Size XL.

**Mall** (`F-BLD-MALL`). A big indoor place to shop and meet.
- 0 None: No mall; shops are on the street. Size -.
- 1 Arcade: One covered arcade with four shops and seating. Size L.
- 2 Full mall: Several floors, many shops, events and crowds. Size XL.

**Hospital and clinic** (`F-BLD-HOSPITAL`). Where health is restored.
- 0 A door: A clinic front you cannot enter. Size S.
- 1 Clinic room: One room where a nurse restores health for a fee. Size M.
- 2 Hospital: A staffed hospital with wards, a medical career and an ambulance. Size XL.

**Workplaces** (`F-BLD-WORK`). Where careers happen.
- 0 Fronts: Offices and workshops are fronts; workers go in and out. Size S.
- 1 One job site: One workplace with real tasks for the player. Size L.
- 2 Several careers: Different workplaces for each career track. Size XL.

**Utility areas** (`F-BLD-UTILITY`). Power, water, waste and depots.
- 0 Set dressing: A depot and pipes as scenery. Size S.
- 1 Bills and workers: Utility workers do rounds; lots get utility bills. Size M.
- 2 Outages and repairs: Things break; districts feel it; repair is a job. Size L.

**Gardens and parks** (`F-GARDEN`). Green places to rest and meet.
- 0 Static garden: A garden with paths and plants that never change. Size S.
- 1 Living garden: Benches people use, seasonal plants, a gardener at work. Size M.
- 2 Community garden: Players plant and tend plots; garden events. Size L.

**Population** (`F-NPC-POP`). How many residents there are and how varied.
- 0 A handful: Five or six people standing at their posts. Size S.
- 1 A neighbourhood: 20 to 30 residents per block, each with a home, a role and a look. Size M.
- 2 A crowd: Hundreds across the city, with cheaper far-away versions. Size XL.

**Daily routines** (`F-NPC-ROUTINE`). What residents do with their day.
- 0 At a post: Each person stays in one place. Size S.
- 1 By the clock: Home, work, meals and leisure by the time of day, a little different each day. Size M.
- 2 By their needs: People choose what to do from their own needs, money and mood. Size XL.

**Week and calendar** (`F-NPC-WEEK`). Whether Tuesday differs from Sunday.
- 0 Every day the same: One routine for every day. Size -.
- 1 Weekdays and weekends: Work days, rest days and market days differ. Size S.
- 2 Festivals: Holidays and seasonal events change the whole town. Size L.
