## What each level means

**City layout** (`F-MAP-CITY`). How much city there is to walk around.
- 0 One block: One hand-built city block with streets around it. Size M.
- 1 A district: Several blocks laid out from lot data, loaded as you move. Size L.
- 2 Whole city: Districts with their own character, streamed seamlessly. Size XL.

**Lots and building records** (`F-MAP-LOTS`). Every plot knows what it is, who owns it and when it is open.
- 0 Hand-placed: Buildings are placed by hand with no data behind them. Size S.
- 1 Registry: A data record per lot drives signs, opening hours, owners and who lives or works there. Size M.
- 2 Changing lots: Lots can change use, be built on or stand empty over time. Size L.

**Going inside buildings** (`F-BLD-INSIDE`). Whether doors lead anywhere.
- 0 Fronts only: Buildings are fronts; people step in and vanish. Size S.
- 1 Key rooms: A few important buildings have one room you can enter. Size L.
- 2 Most buildings: Most buildings have furnished interiors. Size XL.
- 3 Every floor, true size: Every building can be entered and every storey reached by its own stairs (and lifts in the towers). Inside is the same size as outside, behind the same windows; each floor is laid out and furnished for what it is (bedrooms upstairs in houses, a flat on each tower floor, offices, shop stores and staff rooms). Floors are built only near the player. Size XL.

**Building designer** (`F-BLD-DESIGN`). Players make a place their own: remodel what they own, or build new on an open plot, in the town's look.
- 0 None: Buildings are as the town made them. Size -.
- 1 Remodel: Change the finishes of a building or unit you own: walls and their colour, brick or stone, roof tiles, windows and doors outside; floors, walls and ceilings inside. Every building is a design in data; choices from priced catalogues within the district's palette; checked, paid through the ledger. Size M.
- 2 Build new: On an empty plot: draw the footprint, set the storeys and each one's height, choose facades, roof, openings, stairs and lift, rooms and finishes; checked for planning, reachability, stairs and rooms; the result joins the estate market. Size XL.
- 3 Free-form and published: L-shapes, courtyards, set-backs, bays and arcades; designs saved as templates and published by creators. Size XL.

**Open plots** (`F-MAP-PLOTS`). Somewhere to build: the town is full today.
- 0 None: Every lot is built on. Size -.
- 1 Plots for sale: A few empty plots with an owner and a price, bought and sold through the estate market. Size S.
- 2 Clear and rebuild: Owners of a whole building may clear it (tenants given notice); plots come up over time. Size M.

**From design to building** (`F-BLD-CONSTRUCT`). Building should take a little time and show in the street.
- 0 Instant: A confirmed design is there at once. Size S.
- 1 Takes days: Hoardings and scaffolding, then the building, over game days by its size; worked out from the clock. Size M.
- 2 Builders at work: Resident builders on site in their day, materials by van, stages you can watch. Size L.

**Shops and market** (`F-BLD-SHOP`). Where money gets spent.
- 0 Decoration: Stalls and shop fronts you cannot use. Size S.
- 1 Buy at a counter: A shopkeeper sells a short list of goods through the ledger. Size M.
- 2 Stock and prices: Shops hold stock that sells down through the day and restocks, prices move with stock within set bounds, the player sells back to them, and the player can rent a market stall and sell to residents. Size XL.

**Mall** (`F-BLD-MALL`). A big indoor place to shop and meet.
- 0 None: No mall; shops are on the street. Size -.
- 1 Arcade: One covered arcade with four shops and seating. Size L.
- 2 Full mall: Several floors, many shops, events and crowds. Size XL.

**Mall rides** (`F-BLD-RIDES`). Something to do at the mall besides shopping: a carousel and coin rides.
- 0 None: No rides. Size -.
- 1 To sit on: A carousel and coin rides that stand still; the player can sit on them. Size S.
- 2 Rides that turn: The carousel turns and the coin rides rock, with music; a go costs a coin; children queue for them. Size M.

**Hospital and clinic** (`F-BLD-HOSPITAL`). Where health is restored.
- 0 A door: A clinic front you cannot enter. Size S.
- 1 Clinic room: One room where a nurse restores health for a fee. Size M.
- 2 Hospital: A staffed hospital with wards, a medical career and an ambulance. Size XL.

**Bank, showroom, library, museum, cinema** (`F-BLD-CIVIC`). Places for errands and evenings out beyond shops and cafes, and where money, cars and culture will later live.
- 0 None: No such places; the town has shops, cafes, a mall and a hospital. Size -.
- 1 Places to visit: A bank, a car showroom, a library, a museum and a cinema (plus a second hospital and mall), each with its own front and furnished interior, staff at work and residents visiting in their day. Size M.
- 2 Working places: They work: accounts and loans at the bank through the ledger, buying a car at the showroom, films on a timetable, exhibitions that change. Size XL.

**Workplaces** (`F-BLD-WORK`). Where careers happen.
- 0 Fronts: Offices and workshops are fronts; workers go in and out. Size S.
- 1 One job site: One workplace with real tasks for the player. Size L.
- 2 Several careers: Different workplaces for each career track. Size XL.

**Utility areas** (`F-BLD-UTILITY`). Power, water, waste and depots.

- 1 Bills and workers: Utility workers do rounds; lots get utility bills. Size M.
- 2 Outages and repairs: Things break; districts feel it; repair is a job. Size L.

**Gardens and parks** (`F-GARDEN`). Green places to rest and meet.

- 1 Living garden: Benches people use, seasonal plants, a gardener at work. Size M.
- 2 Community garden: Players plant and tend plots; garden events. Size L.

**Street dressing** (`F-MAP-DRESS`). Squares, parks and streets that look lived in: things to look at, sit on and play on, and signs that say what each building is.
- 0 None: As now: lamps, benches, flowerbeds and one small fountain in each park, court and square. Size -.
- 1 Dressed places: Objects placed as data in the block files, drawn in the town's look: playgrounds in the four big parks (swings, slide, seesaw, sandpit, climbing frame), a statue and a grand fountain on the squares, a bandstand in one park; hanging signs, awnings, painted wall adverts and hoardings, window boxes on buildings; bollards, bins, post boxes, bicycle racks, planters, notice boards, a news kiosk and a telephone box along streets; crates, barrows and bunting at the market. - 2 Used and changing: Children play on the swings, slide and seesaw; residents read notice boards and buy at the kiosk; posters and cinema boards change with what is on; bunting only on market days; decorations change with the season. Size L.

- 1 By the clock: Home, work, meals and leisure by the time of day, a little different each day. Size M.
- 2 By their needs: People choose what to do from their own needs, money and mood. Size XL.

**Week and calendar** (`F-NPC-WEEK`). Whether Tuesday differs from Sunday.
- 0 Every day the same: One routine for every day. Size -.
- 1 Weekdays and weekends: Work days, rest days and market days differ. Size S.
- 2 Festivals: Holidays and seasonal events change the whole town. Size L.

- 1 Placed by routine: People are placed from their routine when a player comes near and removed when all leave. Size M.
- 2 Distance tiers: Full, simple and data-only versions by distance, within a server budget, for many players. Size L.

**Talking with residents** (`F-NPC-TALK`). What happens when you speak to someone.

- 1 Dialogue: Conversations with choices and outcomes, standing or walking. Size L.
- 2 Residents talk to each other: People chat among themselves and news spreads. Size L.

**Faces while talking** (`F-NPC-SPEECH`). Whether a speaker's mouth and face move with what they say.

- 2 Lip sync: Mouth shapes timed to each sound of the line (from its text, or from voice if Q-14 says so), expressions blended over time, nods and glances on stressed words. Size L.

**Reacting to the world** (`F-NPC-REACT`). Whether people notice rain, night and you.
- 0 No reaction: People ignore weather and time. Size -.
- 1 Weather and night: People dress for the weather (coats and hoods on cold or wet days, umbrellas up in rain), hurry for cover when rain starts and wait it out under awnings or indoors; lamps and windows light up; people glance at you. Size L.
- 2 Events: People gather, flee or help when something happens. Size L.

- 2 Dodge and tell off: Once clear, they turn on the driver: a raised fist or arms flung up and a short shout (a speech tag, no words spoken, Q-14), then go back to what they were doing. Size S.

**Furniture company and outlets** (`F-FURN-STORE`). Somewhere to see furniture full size, laid out in rooms, before buying it, and a company that sells it through the ledger.
