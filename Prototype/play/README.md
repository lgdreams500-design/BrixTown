# Playable slice

Walk the starting city in third or first person. Open `index.html` in Edge or Chrome (double-click it).
Nothing to install; it runs from these files.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The page and its HUD |
| `play.js` | Builds the city from the data and runs the game |
| `conversations.js` | Talking to residents: the conversation panel, run from the dialogue data |
| `car_models.js` | The Soft box cars as low-poly meshes, near and far. Generated by `Tools/blender/car_export_11.py`; do not edit |
| `cars.js` | Cars, vans and the tram: placed from each vehicle's day in the data (worked out by `Tools/citysim/traffic.py`), the Soft box car models from `car_models.js` (box cars if that file is missing), the people riding in them, and the player driving any parked car |
| `smash.js` | Crash damage (CITY-072 to CITY-076): street things, trees and building corners a fast car breaks, the toppling and the flying pieces, damage saved in the browser, repair crews (the rule of `Tools/citysim/repairs.py`), residents shouting |
| `sound.js` | All the sound, made in code from `World/rules/sound.json` (no audio files): street air, birds and crickets, fountains, footsteps, car engines, the tram and its bell, residents' voices and murmur, action sounds, music by time of day, café, shop and busker music, the car radio with music and podcasts |
| `economy.js` | Money, shops, the market stall, quests and jobs: a port of `Tools/citysim/economy.py` and `quests.py`, checked against them each time the page loads (the console says whether it matches) |
| `rewards.js` | Coins and chimes when money comes in (SND-019, SND-020): a quest or job reward springs coins round the player that fly to the purse; the bank's interest slides out a slip. Reads the ledger, never changes it; checked against `world.reward_events()` each time the page loads |
| `achievements.js` | Achievements (QST-009): a port of `Tools/citysim/achievements.py`, checked against it each time the page loads; earned from facts about the player, kept in the save, listed on the Me tab |
| `dev.js` | Dev mode, `index.html?dev`: a bar of buttons down the left that play each piece of feedback (reward coins of each size, interest, every achievement banner, each reward sound). Moves no money and earns nothing. The \` key frees the mouse for the buttons; a click on the town takes it back |
| `feedback_samples.html` | The samples the owner chose the reward sounds and animations from (Q1, I1 and A1 chosen; ADR-0043) |
| `life.js` | The character designer, the money line and quest tracker, the bank, shop and stall counters, the quest log, quests and jobs in conversation, and the save in the browser (ADR-0012) |
| `voices.js`, `audio/voices/` | Spoken dialogue (SND-018): every line voiced in advance by `Tools/voice/make_dialogue_voices.py`, in each speaker's voice from `World/rules/voices.json` and in the line's mood. You speak your answers too |
| `cast_voices.html` | The cast's voice samples the owner chose from, with each voice in every mood |
| `jukebox.html` | Every music track (score, café, shop, busker, radio), played on its own by `sound.js` so it can be heard and approved |
| `boards.js` | Skateboards (CITY-098 to CITY-100): which kinds the player owns, the board in use, carried or ridden, and the speed rule from `World/rules/boards.json`; `people.js` draws the riding pose (the manual board's push) and the board underfoot or in the hand |
| `people.js` | The people: option B bodies and faces, built part for part as `Tools/blender/character_face_05.py` builds the reference lineup, and the walk from the movement signatures. `index.html?lineup=1` stands the six reference people in a row on Linden Street to compare; `?studio=1` (to `6`) shows one of them under the light and camera of the reference lineup |
| (sky) | The sky and its clouds are drawn by `play.js` from `World/rules/sky.json`: blue overhead, hazy horizon, clouds placed by the world clock (ADR-0014); cloud, rain and fog follow the day's weather (ADR-0016); the planet the town's moon goes round hangs in the north-east, pale by day and out at night (WLD-065, ADR-0040; `sky.json` "planet", `show: false` takes it out) |
| `city_seasons.js` | How each season looks, for the Y key: daylight hours, weather, trees and flowers on the same day of each season. Generated: do not edit |
| `city_data.js` | The city and one day of everyone's routine, packed from `World/`. Generated: do not edit |

## Keys
| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| W A S D, mouse | Walk and look (click the picture to capture the mouse, Esc to let go) |
| Shift | Run |
| Space | Jump |
| Y | Skateboard (CITY-099): get on the board in your hand, or step off and pick it up. No walk on a board: W goes (the manual board only while a foot pushes it, the electric and Neon Boost boards by themselves), the keys steer, letting go glides, S brakes; the board and the speed show above the prompt. `?auto=1` owns all three kinds (manual 50, electric 100, Neon Boost 250 km/h) with the electric board in use; Belongings (G) picks another. `?auto=1&studio=2&board=manual&push=1&turn=90` shows a reference person on a board from the side (`electric`, `neon_boost`; `&carry=1` with it in the hand) |
| V | Switch between third and first person |
| Mouse wheel | Move the third-person camera in or out |
| E | Sit, lie down, get up, ride a lift, talk to the resident in front of you |
| B | At a counter: buy and sell in a shop or café, bank at Linden Bank, run your stall on Market Square |
| I | Your money, what you carry, and your statement |
| J | Quests: what is under way, who has something for you and where they are now, what you finished, who you know |
| 1 to 9, or click | Answer in a conversation (the mouse is let go while you talk) |
| Esc | Leave a conversation |
| F, R | In a car: horn; car radio (three music stations, two podcasts with captions, then off) |
| N | Sound on or off (remembered) |
| [ and ] | Slow or speed the clock (×1, ×5, ×20, ×100) |
| F9 | Try the next season: spring, summer, autumn, winter. The page loads again in the same spot at the same hour, without touching the save. Daylight hours, that season's weather, trees and flowers change; everyone's routine, the cars and the notice boards stay those of the built day. `?season=Winter` starts with one |
| G | Switch off the bounced light and contact shade (ADR-0018) to see the flat shade the slice had before; press again to bring them back. `?gi=0` starts without them |
| O | Switch off the corner shade worked out from the screen's depth (ambient occlusion, ADR-0028); press again to bring it back. `?d_ao=0` starts without it, `?d_ao=2` shows it alone |
| K | Try the weather: steps through clear, cloud, rain and fog, then back to the day's own weather. For looking only; the day itself does not change. `?weather=rain` starts with one |
| T | Skip an hour |
| M | Whole-city map with every resident on it |
| Q | Softer picture without shadows, for a slow machine (it also switches on by itself below 27 frames a second) |
| H | Hide the key list |

## A new game
The first time, **Walk into town** opens the character designer; after that it offers to carry on with the saved character, or to make a new one (which starts afresh). The save is kept in the browser. `index.html?auto=1` skips the designer and never touches the save. The first quest, *New in town*, starts at once: meet Mei at the Tram Café. Money, prices, quests and jobs are data in `World/rules/economy.json`, `quests.json` and `designer.json`.

Frame rate: the picture's sharpness follows the frame rate, aiming for 60 frames a second (a step softer while frames run late, sharper again with room to spare, never so soft that shadows go); **Q** fixes it by hand and `?quality=1` pins it. People far away are cheaper bodies: full detail for the player and the nearest resident within 5 m, a mid body to 16 m, a far body to 35 m, a figure of blocks beyond; mid and far bodies are built every second and third frame and moved in between. `index.html?perf=1` shows under the frame rate what each part of a frame costs on this machine: the script, and on the graphics card the shadows, the depth pass, the corner shade, the lit town and the people and cars.

## After changing the city
```
cd Tools/worldgen
python -m unittest test_world
python build_play.py          # or: python build_play.py 30   for another day of the year
```
Then reload the page.

What is real, what is missing, what was tested and how the look follows the art documents: `docs/specs/domains/WORLD_MAP.md` §5.
