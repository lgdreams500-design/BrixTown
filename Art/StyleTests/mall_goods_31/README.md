# Mall goods 31: the mall's goods for the home, made in Blender (FURN-065, ADR-0041)

The owner, 2026-11-24: "use blender to create the objects, some of these object can be placed in the house like guitar,
books etc." Made by `Tools/blender/mall_goods_31.py` (headless; it builds on furniture kit 17). Not approved: for the
owner to judge.

## The objects alone (Blender, kit daylight, a person for scale)

## What to look at critically

- Colour: the Blender sheets use the furniture kit's light and come out paler than the slice. The slice's colours are
  the ones the game has.
- The far make of the guitars and the drum kit is crude close up, which is why the outlets show the near make.
- Thin parts (the stands' legs, the candlesticks, the television's aerial) are near the 4 cm floor of the interior
  rules; some rods are thinner than that.
- No textures: a book has no title, a clock no numbers, a globe has blocks of land.
