# Light kinds 54 (F-LIGHTS; FURN-066, FURN-067; ADR-0065, ADR-0067)

The approved home room at night, lit by one fitting at a time, each in its named colour from `World/rules/lights.json`.
Made by `Tools/blender/light_kinds_54.py`; the sheet by `Tools/blender/light_kinds_54_sheet.py`. Proposed as the reference
`IndoorLightKinds` (`Art/Reference/catalog.json`), which lists what is seen wrong in it.

These are review pictures at low quality (half size, 24 samples), 2026-11-26.

| Picture | What it is |
|---|---|
| `LightKinds_sheet.jpg` | Every fitting, twice: at one exposure for all, and with the eye set for that light |
| `Fixed_<Fitting>.jpg` | One fitting lit, at the exposure of the room's lamp-lit night, so strengths can be told apart |
| `Eye_<Fitting>.jpg` | The same with the eye opened or closed for that light, at most 2.5 stops |
| `Own_NightLamps.jpg` | The room's own floor lamp, table lamp and fire: the exposure the others are set by |
| `Lines_<Sheet>.jpg` | The fittings as objects in the four style lines (`WASTED_SHEET=1`): Standing, Hung, Street; proposed as `OrnamentsLightLines` |
| `Corner_<Fitting>.jpg` | One fitting lit alone at night in a plain corner, to show how its light spreads (`WASTED_VIG=1`) |
| `light_kinds_54_log.txt` | The run's log: each light's strength, colour and the eye's stops |

Tried and dropped: neon tubes of 30 to 40 W alone in the room (the whole room one colour); candles in a colour of
their own.
