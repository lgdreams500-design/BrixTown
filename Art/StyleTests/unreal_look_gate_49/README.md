# Unreal: the look gate's first use, 2026-11-26

The owner, playing: Eastgate's court "bland ... the structure in the mall they lack depth shadow etc.", and that no
session had noticed. The look gate (`docs/process/LOOK_GATE.md`, ADR-0058) is the answer to the second; these pictures
are its first use. The bar is `Art/Reference/` and ART_DIRECTION's rules, not the browser slice.
